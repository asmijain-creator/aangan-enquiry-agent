What happens on every call

  1. Vaani answers and asks the qualifying questions one at a time, in English or Hinglish. It never quotes a price.
  2. When the call ends, Vaani sends the transcript and 15 extracted fields (including a verdict) to /api/vaani-webhook.
  3. Gemini writes a 20-second handoff note for the designer: who, project, timeline, decision-maker, the caller's own words, open questions.
  4. HubSpot gets a contact and a deal in the "Aangan Enquiries" pipeline, with every field and the note.
  5. Telegram posts the lead to the designers' group with Claim, Consultation booked and Not a fit buttons. Every tap updates HubSpot.
  6. Nikhil's dashboard (private link) shows calls, qualified leads, bookings, wins and the cost per lead.
