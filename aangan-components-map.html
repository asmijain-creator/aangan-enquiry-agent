The cut: no price quotes

pricing.md says “indicative only, varies by site”, so the AI collects area and scope and the designer gives the estimate.

Why Telegram

Instant on the designer’s phone, a Claim button so no lead is missed or doubled, and every tap feeds the dashboard.

Running cost

Measured ₹17 for a 2-minute call (≈₹8/min): about ₹5k a month for 200 calls. Gemini ≈ ₹0.05 a note; HubSpot, Telegram and Vercel are free.

Not built now: WhatsApp and the web form plug in at the same Input step, and everything after stays the same. A Telegram recap to callers was dropped because a bot can’t message a phone number first.
